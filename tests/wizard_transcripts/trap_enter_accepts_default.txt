Q7. Final output format.
    Recommended: SRT — SRT is the safest general-purpose choice.

    1) SRT  — most compatible; best for Plex, Smart TV, tablet apps.
    2) ASS  — best desktop-player control for layout and font size.
    3) VTT  — best for browser/asbplayer Japanese ruby; local players vary.
    4) SMI  — legacy Korean SAMI; font size is usually player-controlled.
    5) TXT  — plain text without timestamps
  Final format [1 | b=back | q=quit] >
